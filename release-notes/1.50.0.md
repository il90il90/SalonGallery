## What's new

**Ten new multi-photo layouts**
Slideshow → Layout now offers Grid, Polaroids, Film strip, Stack, Fan, Gallery wall, Clothesline, Bubbles, Magazine and Columns, next to Mosaic and Scatter. Random mixes single photos with all of them. Every photo always stays fully on the screen.

**Photos appear one by one (optional)**
Turn it on under Layout and each photo of a multi-photo slide fades in a moment after the one before.

**Gentle motion**
Slideshow → Motion adds a subtle movement while a photo waits: a slow Zoom, a soft Drift, a Fade down and back, or Mix. Pick Slow, Medium or Fast.

**Smarter photos**
- Upside-down and sideways photos are straightened automatically, including photos that were already on the frame.
- When a photo has to be cropped to fill the screen or a cell, the crop keeps the faces in view instead of the middle of the picture.
- Mosaic no longer squeezes photos into thin strips that cut faces in half.

**Fixes**
- Scatter on a Hebrew (right-to-left) screen: the prints no longer slide off the edge.
