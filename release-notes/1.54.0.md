## What's new

**One clear "Add" menu**
"Photos & video" and "Whole folder" were two buttons doing nearly the same thing. They're now one: tap Photos & video and choose Photos, Videos, Photos & videos, or a whole folder.

**Much better auto-straightening of upside-down photos**
Orientation now uses a far stronger on-device face detector (ML Kit). Upside-down and sideways photos — including children and faces the old detector missed — are now turned the right way up before they reach the wall. Your whole library is re-checked once after the update.
