## What's new

**Stronger auto-straightening of photos**
The face detector now looks at photos in much higher detail, so small, far-away faces (like one child across the room) are found and the photo is turned the right way up. It only turns a photo when the faces are genuinely upright, so a correct photo of a child hanging upside-down is left alone. Photos an earlier version turned the wrong way are corrected automatically — but anything you rotated by hand is kept. Your library is re-checked once after the update.
