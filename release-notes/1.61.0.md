## What's new

**"Art" is now "Explore"**
The tile is renamed to Explore — it offers free art, space, backgrounds and museum photos, not just art.

**New "Overlays" panel (was "Text")**
The Text tile is now Overlays, redesigned into clear sections: Text, Clock & date, and Weather.

**More clock styles**
Digital, Bold, Card, Minimal, Mono, LED and Analog — pick the look, size and position.

**Pick a font for your text**
Classic, Modern, Mono, Elegant or Round.

**Weather on the wall — free, no account**
Turn on Weather, search your city, and the current temperature shows on the screen. Uses Open-Meteo, which needs no API key at all.
