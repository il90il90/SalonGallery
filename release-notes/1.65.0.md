## What's new

**Sync a folder**
Pick Whole folder once; after that, "Sync folder" re-scans it and adds only the new photos and videos.

**No more duplicates**
Sending the same photo or video twice (or re-syncing a folder) no longer makes a second copy.

**Choose the background colour**
Slideshow → Background lets you set the colour behind a photo — Black, Charcoal, Slate, Warm or White — so a bright white backing no longer lights up the room.
